## Abstract

Knowledge distillation (KD) trains compact student models from larger teachers. Because students learn simpler surface-level features in early layers, errors grow for groups whose labels spuriously correlate with input attributes, degrading group fairness even when overall accuracy is comparable. Early-Exit Neural Networks (EENNs) enable predictions at multiple intermediate layers; confidence margins from early exits have been used to reweight cross-entropy and distillation losses per instance. This paper proposes using Laplace-approximation-based, well-calibrated uncertainty estimates (rather than margins) to reweight hard/ambiguous instances and improve group fairness. They benchmark a BERT-based model on MultiNLI.
