## One-liner

Fair, sparse winning tickets exist and can be identified in one shot without training, when importance is scored by a fairness-aware criterion.

## Key Finding

Iterative magnitude pruning using a fairness-aware importance score finds sparse subnetworks that preserve (or improve) fairness while retaining accuracy.

## Implications / Insights

Fairness can be embedded in the selection of a sparse subnetwork at initialization, so compression need not be a fairness tax if the right subnetwork is chosen.

## Relevance to Us

Shows a sparse-subnetwork (compressed) model can be fair by construction at initialization, analogous to choosing a fair early-exit subnetwork.

## Abstract

Extends the lottery-ticket hypothesis to fairness, searching for fair sparse subnetworks ('fair scratch tickets') that are both accurate and fair without weight training, using a fairness-aware weight importance.
