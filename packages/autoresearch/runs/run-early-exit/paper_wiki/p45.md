## One-liner

Quantization reshapes LLM social bias unevenly across demographic subgroups and architectures, so bias cannot be assumed constant under compression.

## Key Finding

Quantization affects subgroups differently, and the direction/scale of bias change depends on model architecture and reasoning capability, complicating safe deployment.

## Abstract

Investigates how quantization influences social bias in LLMs across different demographic subgroups, model architectures, and reasoning capability levels; finds quantization shifts bias in subgroup-specific and architecture-dependent ways.
