## One-liner

Frames bias as a differential in prediction quality/accuracy across social groups, not just association likelihood.

## Key Finding

Models show differential prediction quality across demographic group tokens, revealing an accuracy/confidence gap tied to group identity that pure preference measures can miss.

## Implications / Insights

Bias can be detected as an asymmetric quality/accuracy gap, aligning model fairness with measured capacity rather than sentiment.

## Relevance to Us

Provides an accuracy-gap methodology for detecting whether early-exit/adaptive LLMs give different prediction quality to different groups.

## Abstract

Proposes measuring social bias in masked LMs through per-group prediction quality (accuracy/confidence on tokens associated with different social groups) rather than only through likelihood preference.
