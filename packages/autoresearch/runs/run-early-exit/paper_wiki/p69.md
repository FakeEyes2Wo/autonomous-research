## One-liner

Transformer layers collapse into a few clusters of similar representations after fine-tuning, not a smooth gradient.

## Key Finding

Fine-tuned layers show block/clustered similarity structure (early, middle, late groups), so representation change is not monotonic and some layer groups are near-redundant.

## Abstract

Measures representational similarity (CKA-style) between all pairs of layers in fine-tuned BERT-like models, revealing that layers organize into clusters of similar representations.
