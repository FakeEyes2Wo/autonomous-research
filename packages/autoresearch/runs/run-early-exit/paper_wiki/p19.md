## One-liner

Guard a few important weight channels, found by activation magnitude, to get high-quality low-bit quantization.

## Key Finding

AWQ quantizes to 4-bit with much better accuracy than prior methods and enables on-device Llama serving with 3x+ speedups and large memory savings.

## Abstract

AWQ identifies a small set of salient weight channels by observing activation magnitudes rather than weight magnitudes, then protects those channels during quantization.
