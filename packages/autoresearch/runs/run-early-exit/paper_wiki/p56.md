## Implications / Insights

Early-exit decisions based purely on entropy (or confidence) may be unsafe, since low-uncertainty tokens can still be incorrect.

## Relevance to Us

Warns that entropy thresholds (a common early-exit rule) are not sufficient to ensure quality parity across groups.

## Abstract

Argues that entropy-based uncertainty is insufficient for safe selective prediction/abstention in LLMs, and that additional signals (e.g., semantic, self-consistency, calibration-aware) are needed.
