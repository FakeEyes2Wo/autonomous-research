## One-liner

Compression does not affect all groups equally, so compressed models can be less fair than the originals.

## Key Finding

Distillation and pruning at aggressive rates disproportionately hurt underrepresented groups even when average performance is maintained, and the bias depends on the compression method.

## Implications / Insights

Compression is a fairness risk even when it preserves mean accuracy, so subgroup-level auditing is required for any model-reduction technique.

## Abstract

Examines how compression (pruning, quantization, distillation) interacts with fairness, showing that compressed models can disproportionately degrade underrepresented groups and that the effect is a function of the compression technique and group.
