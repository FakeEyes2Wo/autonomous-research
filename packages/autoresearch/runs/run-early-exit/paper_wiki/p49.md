## One-liner

Distillation is not fairness-neutral — it can amplify or reduce bias depending on teacher/student and settings.

## Key Finding

Distilling a teacher into a smaller student shifts bias meaningfully, and the direction depends on distillation hyperparameters, teacher bias, and student capacity.

## Implications / Insights

Model reduction via distillation must be audited for fairness, because the smaller model can carry more (not less) bias than the teacher.

## Relevance to Us

## Abstract

Empirically studies how knowledge distillation affects fairness and bias, showing distillation can substantially change (and sometimes amplify) bias depending on teacher, student, and training setup; questions the assumption distillation is fairness-neutral.
