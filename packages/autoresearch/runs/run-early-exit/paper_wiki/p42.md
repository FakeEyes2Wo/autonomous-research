## One-liner

Sparsity and disparate-impact constraints are enforced together, giving sparse models that stay fair by construction.

## Key Finding

One can attain high sparsity while holding disparate impact near the pre-pruning level, but there is a real sparsity-versus-fairness trade-off a joint method navigates far better than post-hoc corrections.

## Implications / Insights

Sparse-model fairness must be a hard constraint during training/compression, not an afterthought, and comes at a measurable sparsity cost.

## Relevance to Us

Introduces the trade-off framing essential for early-exit LLMs: each exit threshold is a compression knob tunable against a fairness constraint.

## Abstract

Develops a method to enforce sparsity and a disparate-impact (fairness) constraint simultaneously, producing sparse models that are both compact and fair, with a principled framework to trade off sparsity vs fairness.
