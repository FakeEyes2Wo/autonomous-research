## One-liner

Adding a fairness loss during pruning keeps accuracy balanced across skin tones instead of letting AUC-ROC drift apart.

## Key Finding

Pruning alone causes divergent AUC-ROC across dark vs light skin tones; a fair pruning loss eliminates that divergence while preserving sparsity.

## Implications / Insights

The pruning objective itself is the correct place to inject fairness, rather than post-hoc rebalancing of the sparse model.

## Relevance to Us

Encapsulates how to make an efficiency transform fairness-aware during the compression step — analogous to making an early-exit policy fairness-aware at training time.

## Abstract

Introduces a fairness-aware loss added during pruning so the pruned network does not diverge in performance across demographic groups, reporting balanced AUC-ROC across skin tones even under heavy pruning.
