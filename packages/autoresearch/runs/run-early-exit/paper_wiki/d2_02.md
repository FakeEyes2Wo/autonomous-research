## Abstract

As LLMs enter sensitive domains, how their confidence scores relate to fairness/bias matters. This study examines alignment between LLM-predicted confidence and human-annotated bias judgments, focusing on gender bias in gendered-pronoun-resolution contexts. It evaluates whether probability-confidence calibration metrics capture fairness-related disparities. Among six state-of-the-art models, Gemma-2 shows the worst calibration on the gender-bias benchmark. The main contribution is a fairness-aware evaluation of LLM confidence calibration plus a new calibration metric, Gender-ECE, designed to measure gender disparities in resolution tasks.
