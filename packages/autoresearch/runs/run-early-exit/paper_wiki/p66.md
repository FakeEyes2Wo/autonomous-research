## One-liner

Gender bias is localized to specific layers and components, so it can be targeted rather than treated uniformly.

## Key Finding

Gender bias concentrates in particular layers and modules (often MLPs and attention at certain depths); removing it there reduces bias with minimal general-performance loss.
