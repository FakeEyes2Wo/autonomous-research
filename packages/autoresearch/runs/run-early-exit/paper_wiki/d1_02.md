## Abstract

Early-Exit Deep Neural Networks allow adaptive inference by predicting at intermediate layers, cutting cost and latency. Most strategies greedily exit when confidence exceeds a threshold fixed on a static validation set, which is problematic because the model can be overconfident in a wrong class and is not robust to distribution shift. The authors propose UAT, which adapts the exit threshold online with a Multi-Armed Bandit framework, using a reward function that assesses predictive certainty and its reliability to balance efficiency and quality while penalizing unnecessary late exits. They provide risk guarantees and validate on vision-language understanding, text generation, and classification, achieving 1.70-2.10x speedup with <2% accuracy drop.
