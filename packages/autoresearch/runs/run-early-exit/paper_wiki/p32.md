## One-liner

Argues for measuring LLM fairness at the group level across outputs, not with a single aggregate number.

## Key Finding

LLMs exhibit measurable group-level disparities in generated-output quality/content that are hidden when only a single pooled fairness value is reported.

## Abstract

Proposes a group-level fairness framework for LLMs evaluating fairness by comparing the distribution/quality of outputs across demographic groups rather than by a single scalar, arguing group-based comparison reveals disparities masked by pooled scores.
