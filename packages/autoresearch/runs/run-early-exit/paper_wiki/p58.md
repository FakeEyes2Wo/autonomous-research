## One-liner

A training objective that improves selective prediction (abstention) and the risk-coverage trade-off in NLP.

## Key Finding

Error-regularized selective prediction substantially improves risk-coverage and calibration, enabling models to abstain on hard inputs while preserving accuracy on confident ones.

## Abstract

Studies selective prediction (abstention) in NLP with a calibration-aware error-regularization training objective, improving the model's ability to abstain when uncertain and improving the risk-coverage trade-off.
