## One-liner

The canonical gendered coreference benchmark revealing a large performance gap on stereotypical vs anti-stereotypical examples.

## Key Finding

Coreference systems perform far worse on anti-stereotypical sentences; data augmentation and gender-balanced embeddings narrow but do not remove the gap.

## Implications / Insights

A model's reliability is not uniform across demographic groups — performance can be tied to stereotype conformance.

## Relevance to Us

A classic template for measuring an accuracy/quality disparity between demographic groups — the kind that adaptive inference could introduce or amplify.

## Abstract

Introduces WinoBias, a coreference-resolution dataset built around stereotypical and anti-stereotypical profession/pronoun pairs to expose gender bias, plus two debiasing methods (gender-balanced embeddings and data augmentation).
