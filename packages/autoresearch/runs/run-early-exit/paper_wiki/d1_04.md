## Abstract

Intermediate-layer predictions in LLMs are informative but hard to decode accurately, especially at early layers. Lens-style methods typically use a direct linear readout that drifts from the model's eventual prediction. SimLens is a training-free decoder for single-token decision tasks that keeps only the start token and a candidate answer token and runs one lightweight continuation through the upper layers, recovering far more accurate latent predictions than direct linear decoding. Linear SimLens approximates entropy-based confidence, and SimExit combines both into a hybrid early-exit mechanism. On ARC, BoolQ, and HeadQA with LLaMA-7B/Vicuna-7B, SimLens improves Iso-Compute accuracy in all six settings (+0.43 average); SimExit yields 1.15x speedup at best-accuracy and 1.40x with up to 1-point accuracy drop.
