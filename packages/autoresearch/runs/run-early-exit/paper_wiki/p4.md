## One-liner

Per-token adaptive depth for generative LLMs, exiting when the current layer's softmax confidence is high enough.

## Key Finding

CALM terminates most tokens after a few layers while matching full-depth quality, using a risk-controlling threshold with statistical guarantees.

## Implications / Insights

Every token's compute depends on the model's self-reported confidence, directly redistributing work per token.

## Relevance to Us

The canonical LLM early-exit method; its per-token confidence-driven depth allocation is exactly the mechanism that could deliver unequal compute to groups.

## Abstract

For decoder-only next-token LMs, CALM dynamically chooses how many layers to run per token based on softmax confidence, with a risk-controlling formulation bounding the probability of premature stopping.
