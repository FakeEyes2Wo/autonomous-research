## Relevance to Us

Removing attention heads or depth layers changes which language patterns are preserved, directly relevant to fairness auditing.

## Abstract

LLM-Pruner performs structural pruning of LLMs by removing coupled structures (attention heads, FFN channels) selected by gradient-based importance in a task-agnostic way, then lightly fine-tunes to recover performance.
