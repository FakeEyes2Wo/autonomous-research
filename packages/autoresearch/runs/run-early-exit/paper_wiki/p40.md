## One-liner

Pruning and fairness are solved together as a single bi-level problem, so the sparse model satisfies both sparsity and group-fairness goals.

## Key Finding

Treating fairness as a constraint inside a bi-level optimization lets pruning reach high sparsity without sacrificing equalized-odds across groups, outperforming post-hoc pruning-then-rebalancing.

## Implications / Insights

Compression and fairness should be co-optimized, not sequential, because pruning first and correcting later tends to entrench disparity.

## Relevance to Us

Provides the optimization machinery to add a demographic-fairness constraint when training early-exit heads, rather than auditing exits after the fact.

## Abstract

Formulates fairness-preserving pruning as a bi-level optimization that jointly decides which weights to drop and enforces a fairness constraint (e.g., equalized odds), asking how to make a compressed model fair in one run.
