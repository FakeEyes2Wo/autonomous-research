## Key Finding

Semantic entropy better distinguishes correct from incorrect generations than token-level metrics, improving selective prediction and could reduce hallucination risk.

## Implications / Insights

Token-level confidence is a poor proxy for generation uncertainty; meaning-aware uncertainty is more reliable.

## Relevance to Us

If generation-uncertainty is meaning-based and differs across groups, then early-exit confidence (often token-level) may mis-rank groups.

## Abstract

Introduces semantic entropy: cluster sampled generations by meaning (via entailment) and compute entropy over semantic equivalence classes, giving a way to estimate uncertainty in open-ended generation.
