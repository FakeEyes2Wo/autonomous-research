## Abstract

LLMs are increasingly deployed in high-impact domains (clinical, legal, hiring, education), making pre-deployment fairness/bias evaluation critical. Existing evaluations are not grounded in real scenarios and ignore harm-severity differences (e.g., a biased surgical decision should not be weighted like a stylistic summarization bias). HALF (Harm-Aware LLM Fairness) assesses model bias in realistic applications and weighs outcomes by harm severity, organizing nine application domains into three tiers (Severe/Moderate/Mild) via a five-stage pipeline. Across eight LLMs, they find (1) LLMs are not consistently fair across domains, (2) model size/performance do not guarantee fairness, and (3) reasoning models do better in medical decision support but worse in education.
