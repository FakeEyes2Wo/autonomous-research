## One-liner

Quantization's effect on multimodal bias is inconsistent across models and precision, so it cannot be assumed harmful or harmless.

## Key Finding

Quantization-induced bias changes vary in direction and magnitude across vision-language models and bit-widths, so no single 'quantization hurts fairness' claim holds.

## Abstract

Systematically studies how quantization affects bias in multimodal vision-language foundation models, finding its impact is neither consistent nor uniform across models, tasks, and precision; some settings amplify bias, others reduce it.
