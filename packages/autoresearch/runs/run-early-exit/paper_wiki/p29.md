## One-liner

The standard dataset and framework for measuring and reducing toxic/unsafe outputs in open-ended generation.

## Key Finding

Pretrained LMs readily produce toxic continuations from fairly neutral prompts, and toxicity rises with generation length and model size; mitigations reduce but do not eliminate it.

## Implications / Insights

Inference-time choices (length, decoding, caching) materially change the risk-profile of generated content, so adaptive inference can shift harm.

## Relevance to Us

Shows inference-time behavior (which early-exit changes) alters harm; a natural outcome variable for measuring uneven quality/harm across demographics.

## Abstract

RealToxicityPrompts is a dataset of 100k naturally occurring web prompts whose continuations are scored for toxicity via the Perspective API, documenting 'toxic degeneration' and evaluating filtering, detoxification, and decoding strategies as mitigation.
