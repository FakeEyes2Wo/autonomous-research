## One-liner

A distillation loss that propagates fairness to a compact student, keeping compressed students group-fair.

## Key Finding

Naive distillation inherits (and can amplify) teacher bias; adding a fairness-aware distillation objective yields fair, compact students without much accuracy loss.

## Implications / Insights

## Relevance to Us

Mirrors the teacher→student bias-transfer question for early-exit nets, where small exit heads distill the backbone's behavior (and biases).

## Abstract

Introduces a fairness-aware knowledge distillation approach for graph neural networks so the distilled (smaller) student stays fair even when the teacher is biased, balancing performance, complexity, and fairness.
