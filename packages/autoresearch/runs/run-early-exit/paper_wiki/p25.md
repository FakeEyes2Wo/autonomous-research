## One-liner

Many of an LLM's deepest layers hardly matter, so you can drop them at inference for a nearly-free speedup.

## Key Finding

Dropping roughly half the deepest layers of a 70B model gives ~1.5x speedup on a consumer GPU with minimal performance loss; some tasks like MMLU improve.

## Abstract

Shows that many of the deepest layers of trained LLMs contribute negligibly at inference, and that dropping them with a cheap layer-selection heuristic preserves performance while enabling faster inference (and sometimes improving tasks like MMLU).
