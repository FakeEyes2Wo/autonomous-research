## One-liner

BERT's layers recapitulate the classical NLP pipeline, with syntax emerging early and semantics later.

## Key Finding

Per-layer probing accuracy organizes into a clear bottom-up order (surface → syntactic → semantic), each task peaking at a characteristic depth and often declining afterward.

## Implications / Insights

Representation 'maturity' is task- and depth-specific, so a fixed-depth readout (including an early exit) captures different information for different inputs.

## Relevance to Us

Supports the premise that layers reach probe-decodable ('mature') representations at different depths, the mechanism behind group-dependent early-exit gaps.

## Abstract

Probes each layer of BERT with lightweight classifiers for syntactic and semantic tasks, showing representations encode information in a pipeline-like bottom-up order.
