## One-liner

Double early exiting within one model to draft and verify tokens losslessly for faster decoding.

## Key Finding

Combining a cheap early-exit draft with a second, more reliable intermediate verification improves acceptance rate and speedup while keeping output lossless.

## Relevance to Us

## Abstract

Uses a lightweight self-drafting mechanism with two early-exit stages — a cheap draft exit and a verification/proposal stage — to accelerate lossless decoding within a single model.
