## Abstract

Scaling LLMs is expensive. Existing efficiency efforts target either parameter sharing or adaptive computation, but rarely both. Mixture-of-Recursions (MoR) combines both axes in a single Recursive Transformer: it reuses a shared stack of layers across recursion steps for parameter efficiency while lightweight routers adaptively assign different recursion depths to individual tokens (adaptive token-level thinking), focusing quadratic attention only among still-active tokens and caching only their KV pairs. A KV-sharing variant reuses KV pairs from the first recursion to cut memory. Across 135M-1.7B scales, MoR forms a new Pareto frontier, lowering validation perplexity and improving few-shot accuracy at equal training FLOPs while boosting throughput vs. vanilla/recursive baselines.
