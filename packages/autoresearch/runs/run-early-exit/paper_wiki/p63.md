## One-liner

Transformer representations specialize bottom-up, with each type of linguistic knowledge emerging at a distinct layer.

## Key Finding

Lower layers encode language-general features that transfer across objectives, while deeper layers encode task-specific information; specialization is incremental and bottom-up.

## Implications / Insights

The 'maturity depth' of a representation depends on the information type, so shallow outputs retain general structure but miss deep task-specific signal.

## Relevance to Us

Core evidence that the content of representations — and hence their maturity — unfolds across depth, enabling depth-dependent group differences.

## Abstract

Studies how representations evolve layer by layer in Transformer MT/LM models, finding different types of linguistic information become separable at different depths and lower layers are more general while deeper are task-specific.
