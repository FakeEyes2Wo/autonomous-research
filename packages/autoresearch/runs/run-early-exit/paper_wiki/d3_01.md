## Abstract

Post-training quantization (PTQ) is widely used for its high compression capacity, speed, and minimal accuracy impact. However, the authors observe that disparate impact is exacerbated by quantization, especially for minority groups. They explain that a chain of factors in forward and backward passes creates disparate impact across groups: changes in weights and activations from quantization cause cascaded network effects, lowering logit variance, increasing loss, and compromising group accuracies. They extend the study to the influence on group gradient norms and Hessian eigenvalues from an optimization perspective. To mitigate, they propose integrating mixed-precision quantization-aware training (QAT) with dataset sampling and weighted loss functions, enabling fairer deployment of quantized networks.
