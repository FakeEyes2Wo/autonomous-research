## One-liner

A large open-ended generation benchmark with concrete per-domain fairness metrics to quantify bias in LLM output text.

## Key Finding

Larger models (GPT-2, GPT-3) display more bias in sentiment, regard, and toxicity toward specific groups than smaller ones, with direction/intensity depending on domain.

## Implications / Insights

Scaling up models is not bias-neutral; quality and 'fairness' signals must be tracked by group during generation.

## Relevance to Us

Supplies per-group sentiment/regard/toxicity metrics to compare whether early-exit LLMs degrade output quality differently across groups.

## Abstract

BOLD is a dataset of 23,679 English Wikipedia prompt templates over five domains (gender, race, religion, political affiliation, profession) with five fairness metrics — sentiment, regard, toxicity, gender polarity, word-level similarity.
