## One-liner

A hand-built QA benchmark measuring how strongly LLMs lean on demographic stereotypes by contrasting disambiguated vs ambiguous settings.

## Key Finding

Large pretrained QA models show substantial bias in ambiguous contexts (often worse than majority/random baselines), expressed as a large accuracy gap between stereotypic and non-stereotypic answers.

## Implications / Insights

A model can look 'fair' on unambiguous inputs yet be strongly biased when information is missing, so fairness must be measured in under-determined settings.

## Relevance to Us

Its accuracy-gap and bias-score framing is the closest template for testing whether an early-exit/adaptive LLM yields unequal QA accuracy across groups.

## Abstract

BBQ is a QA benchmark of ~25k questions across nine social-bias categories, each in disambiguated and ambiguous contexts, scoring whether models rely on stereotypes when the answer is not inferable.
