## One-liner

A training-free, hash-based early-exit controller keyed on the token itself rather than learned confidence.

## Key Finding

A simple token hash is surprisingly competitive, showing much of the speedup comes from skipping layers on predictable tokens rather than sophisticated confidence estimators.

## Implications / Insights

The exit signal being a function of token identity means compute allocation is purely a function of surface token, independent of model confidence.

## Relevance to Us

Shows the exit decision need not rely on model confidence, sharpening the point that any input-dependent policy can yield unequal group compute.

## Abstract

Uses a cheap hash function over token embeddings to decide early exit for each token, making early exiting nearly free and applicable to both understanding and generation.
