## Abstract

This work comprehensively evaluates how quantization affects model bias, with attention to individual demographic subgroups. Focusing on weight and activation quantization strategies, it examines effects across a broad range of bias types — stereotypes, fairness, toxicity, sentiment — using both probability- and generated-text-based metrics across 13 benchmarks and models differing in architecture family and reasoning ability. Findings: quantization has a nuanced impact on bias — it can reduce toxicity and does not significantly affect sentiment, but tends to slightly increase stereotypes and unfairness in generative tasks, especially under aggressive compression. Trends are generally consistent across demographic categories/subgroups and model types, though magnitude depends on the specific setting.
