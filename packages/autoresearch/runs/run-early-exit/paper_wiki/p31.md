## One-liner

A minimal-pair benchmark scoring social bias by comparing model likelihood of stereotypical vs anti-stereotypical sentences.

## Key Finding

All tested pretrained masked LMs show measurable social bias favoring stereotypical sentences, with magnitude varying by category and model.

## Implications / Insights

Bias is present even in models not explicitly designed for generation, and is a property of learned associations, not just decode-time choices.

## Relevance to Us

A lightweight group-bias probe adaptable to checking whether early-exit thresholds alter the relative likelihood of stereotypical completions.

## Abstract

CrowS-Pairs is a benchmark of 1,508 minimal sentence pairs (one stereotypical, one anti-stereotypical) across nine bias categories, scored by the relative likelihood a masked LM assigns to the stereotypical sentence.
