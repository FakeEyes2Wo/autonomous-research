## One-liner

Gender information is concentrated in particular BERT layers, and layer-wise probing localizes where stereotypes live.

## Key Finding

Gender bias is unevenly distributed across layers; a subset of layers is far more predictive of gender and stereotype associations, i.e., the bias 'matures' at specific depths.

## Implications / Insights

## Relevance to Us

Core evidence that a demographic attribute's representation becomes separable (mature) only at certain depths, implying group-specific fairness effects at early-exit cutoffs.

## Abstract

Measures how gender information is distributed across BERT's layers by probing each layer for gender and stereotypical associations, localizing where bias is strongest.
